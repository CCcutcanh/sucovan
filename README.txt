BAO TANG AO TAY SON - BAN WINDOWS DA SUA

BAT DAU SU DUNG
1. Giai nen TOAN BO thu muc nay vao mot vi tri co quyen ghi (vi du Desktop hoac Documents, khong nen dat trong Program Files).
2. Bam dup BatTangTaySon-Windows.bat.
3. Bao tang mo trong cua so ung dung Edge. Can Internet de tai thu vien Three.js va phông chu.
4. Chinh sua noi dung nhu binh thuong. Trong cua so Quan ly bao tang, bam nut 💾 Luu ngay o phia tren (hoac nhan Ctrl+S) de ghi du lieu vao museum-data.json ngay trong thu muc du an. Cac thay doi van tu dong luu nen nut nay la cach luu chac chan va de kiem tra.
5. Khi chuyen sang may khac, dong bao tang, sao chep TOAN BO thu muc du an (bao gom museum-data.json neu da tao) sang may moi, roi bam BatTangTaySon-Windows.bat.

VIDEO TV SA HINH
- RGXM.mp4: chien dich Rach Gam – Xoai Mut (quan Xiêm).
- NH-DD.mp4: chien dich Ngoc Hoi – Dong Da (quan Thanh).
- Video da duoc chuyen sang MP4 H.264 de tuong thich voi Edge/Chrome tren Windows.
- TV tu dong phat video khi vao khong gian tham quan 3D. Video mac dinh tat tieng de tuan theo chinh sach autoplay cua trinh duyet; nguoi dung co the bat tieng bang nut dieu khien video neu can.
- Hai video mac dinh doc truc tiep tu thu muc, khong phu thuoc vao localStorage hay thao tac tai video len trong Quan ly.

LUU VA CHUYEN NOI DUNG
- Nut 💾 Luu ngay va phim tat Ctrl+S ghi noi dung vao museum-data.json. Sau khi hien thong bao da luu thanh cong, co the dong ung dung.
- Khi chay bang launcher Windows, noi dung chinh sua duoc ghi vao museum-data.json, khong chi nam trong bo nho trinh duyet.
- De chuyen may, phai sao chep ca thu muc, khong chi sao chep file HTML.
- Neu muon chia se file HTML rieng, su dung Quan ly > Luu va chia se > Tai bo file de chia se. Neu trinh duyet hoi quyen tai nhieu tep, hay cho phep; giu HTML, CSS, JS, hai video va anh trong cung thu muc.

TRO GIUP
- Neu Windows hien canh bao script, chi chay file neu ban biet day la goi bao tang cua minh; launcher chi chay may chu noi bo tren 127.0.0.1 va mo bao tang.
- De dung may chu noi bo, dong cua so PowerShell co tieu de BAO TANG AO TAY SON.
- Khong xoa museum-data.json neu muon giu noi dung da sua.
